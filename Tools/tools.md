# AI-Powered Literature Discovery Tools

This section presents five AI-powered tools that can support researchers in literature discovery, information retrieval, source analysis, research synthesis, and academic writing. The selected tools include both general-purpose AI research assistants and specialized scholarly literature platforms.

## 1. ChatGPT

**Type:** General-purpose AI research assistant
**Developer:** OpenAI

**Purpose:**
ChatGPT can assist researchers with understanding research topics, developing research questions, summarizing and comparing information, organizing literature, identifying research gaps, and preparing structured research outputs.

**Key Features:**

* Research question development
* Literature summarization and comparison
* Source analysis
* Information synthesis
* Research brainstorming
* Structured report generation
* Citation-supported research workflows

**Relevance to Literature Discovery:**
ChatGPT can help researchers explore unfamiliar topics, develop search strategies, compare sources, and organize information discovered during the literature-review process.

**Official Website:**
[ChatGPT](https://chatgpt.com/)

**Research Guide:**
[ChatGPT for Research](https://openai.com/academy/research/)

---

## 2. NotebookLM (Gemini Notebook)

**Type:** AI-powered source-grounded research assistant
**Developer:** Google

**Purpose:**
NotebookLM is an AI research assistant designed to help users understand and organize information from their own sources. Users can provide sources such as PDFs, websites, YouTube videos, audio files, Google Docs, and Google Slides and interact with the resulting notebook.

**Key Features:**

* Upload and organize research sources
* Source-grounded question answering
* Summarization
* Inline source citations
* Study guides and briefings
* Audio overviews
* Mind maps and other generated research formats

**Relevance to Literature Discovery:**
NotebookLM is useful when a researcher has already collected papers or other sources and wants to understand, compare, summarize, and connect information across them.

**Official Website:**
[NotebookLM / Gemini Notebook](https://notebooklm.google.com/)

**Documentation:**
[Google NotebookLM Help](https://support.google.com/notebooklm/)

---

## 3. Perplexity

**Type:** AI-powered search and research assistant
**Developer:** Perplexity

**Purpose:**
Perplexity combines conversational AI with web search to help users investigate research questions and locate relevant sources. Its Research mode performs multiple searches and synthesizes information into a research report.

**Key Features:**

* AI-powered web search
* Source-linked answers
* Research mode
* Multi-step research
* Source comparison
* Report generation
* Follow-up research questions

**Relevance to Literature Discovery:**
Perplexity can help researchers perform initial topic exploration, identify relevant sources, discover terminology, and investigate research questions before conducting more focused scholarly searches.

**Official Website:**
[Perplexity](https://www.perplexity.ai/)

**Research Mode:**
[Perplexity Research](https://www.perplexity.ai/help-center/en/articles/10738684-what-is-research-mode)

---

## 4. Elicit

**Type:** AI-powered academic research and literature-review tool
**Developer:** Elicit

**Purpose:**
Elicit is designed specifically for scientific research. It helps researchers search academic literature, identify relevant papers, analyze research findings, extract information, and organize evidence for literature reviews.

**Key Features:**

* Semantic academic search
* Research reports
* Paper screening
* Data extraction
* Systematic literature-review support
* Research libraries
* Research alerts

**Relevance to Literature Discovery:**
Elicit is directly relevant to AI-powered literature discovery because it searches academic papers using semantic search and helps researchers identify and analyze evidence. Its systematic-review features can also support screening and data extraction.

**Official Website:**
[Elicit](https://elicit.com/)

**Research Reports:**
[Elicit Reports](https://elicit.com/)

---

## 5. Semantic Scholar

**Type:** AI-powered academic search and discovery platform
**Developer:** Allen Institute for AI (AI2)

**Purpose:**
Semantic Scholar is a free AI-powered research tool for discovering scientific literature. It provides access to a large collection of scholarly papers and uses AI techniques to help researchers find relevant research and explore connections between papers.

**Key Features:**

* AI-powered academic search
* Paper recommendations
* Citation and reference exploration
* Related-paper discovery
* Author and research-topic exploration
* Semantic Reader
* Research API

**Relevance to Literature Discovery:**
Semantic Scholar is directly focused on scientific literature discovery. It helps researchers find relevant papers, explore citations and references, identify related research, and understand scholarly connections.

**Official Website:**
[Semantic Scholar](https://www.semanticscholar.org/)

**API:**
[Semantic Scholar API](https://www.semanticscholar.org/product/api)

## Comparison of Tools

| Tool                 | Primary Focus                  | Main Strength                              | Best Used For                                     |
| -------------------- | ------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------- |
| **ChatGPT**          | General AI research assistance | Research planning and synthesis            | Understanding, comparing, and organizing research |
| **NotebookLM**       | Source-grounded research       | Working with your own sources              | Summarizing and connecting collected papers       |
| **Perplexity**       | AI-powered web search          | Fast source discovery and research reports | Initial research and web-based exploration        |
| **Elicit**           | Academic literature research   | Literature search and evidence extraction  | Literature reviews and systematic research        |
| **Semantic Scholar** | Scholarly search               | Scientific paper and citation discovery    | Finding academic papers and related research      |

## Summary

The five tools provide complementary approaches to AI-assisted research. ChatGPT is useful for research planning, analysis, and synthesis, while NotebookLM focuses on understanding and connecting information from user-provided sources. Perplexity combines AI with web search for rapid research and source discovery. Elicit is specialized for academic literature search and evidence extraction, while Semantic Scholar focuses on scientific-paper discovery and citation relationships.

These differences demonstrate that AI-powered research tools are not identical: some are general-purpose assistants, while others are specifically designed for scholarly literature discovery and evidence-based research.
