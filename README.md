## Topic Overview

The rapid growth of scientific publications has made it increasingly difficult for researchers and students to discover, evaluate, and organize relevant academic literature. Traditional keyword-based searches can return large numbers of results while making it difficult to identify the most relevant papers, related research, citation relationships, and emerging topics. AI-powered literature discovery tools attempt to address these challenges by using techniques such as natural language processing, semantic search, machine learning, citation analysis, document embeddings, and generative AI.

These technologies can support researchers at different stages of the literature-review process. Tools such as Semantic Scholar and Elicit focus on academic literature discovery and analysis, while systems such as ChatGPT, NotebookLM, and Perplexity provide broader AI-assisted research capabilities. Citation networks and scientific-document representations can also help researchers identify related studies and understand connections within a research field.

However, AI-assisted discovery also introduces challenges. Researchers must verify sources, check citation accuracy, evaluate the quality and relevance of generated information, and remain aware of hallucinations and biases. The purpose of this repository is therefore not only to collect AI research tools but also to provide verified scholarly literature, datasets, implementations, and learning resources that support responsible and reproducible research.

## Responsible Research and Verification

AI tools can accelerate literature discovery and research workflows, but AI-generated information should not automatically be considered reliable.

Before including a scholarly source in this repository, researchers should verify:

* Paper title
* Authors
* Publication year
* Journal or conference
* DOI or legitimate source link
* Existence of the publication
* Relevance to the research topic

This repository therefore emphasizes **verification, transparency, responsible AI use, and reproducibility**.
